# Release notes

Newest first. Each entry is the text published with that release on the App Store and Google Play.

## 1.4.1

Fills now land the way a drummer plays them: the groove keeps going until the fill starts, instead of dropping out for half a bar first.

## 1.4

### Fills, heard where they go

Press and hold Fill for a fill every 2, 4 or 8 bars. The last bar of each phrase plays a made-up fill and the grid shows it as it sounds; press the lit chip to stop. Nothing is written to your groove.

### Learn it

In the Practice sheet, one chip starts a groove slower and climbs back to its own tempo, five at a time every four bars, count-in included. It stops when it gets there.

### Rudiment stickings in one press

The R/L label's menu now writes doubles and the paradiddle across the row, starting with either hand, beside the all-right, all-left and alternating fills it already had.

### Snare on 2 and 4, kick on 1 and 3

Every row's sheet fills On, Down, Up, 1/3, 2/4, Double or Shuffle, and each chip presses off again, so the skeleton of a beat is two presses and a wrong guess is one more.

### Easier on the battery

With the screen off, Tating used to keep the phone awake asking for frames it never drew. It sleeps properly now.

## 1.3

### The drum names read as names

Down the left of the grid, each row is now its initials beside a slim stripe of its color, instead of a filled block. A filled block is a solid shape sitting in a page of notation, which is exactly what a note looks like. The names are quieter now, and the notes are the only thing filled in.

### A Holidays filter

The library's filter row has a Holidays chip, so the seasonal grooves are one tap away instead of somewhere inside the styles.

### Clearer about what you are buying

On the purchase card, the unlock and the Holidays pack each sit above their own button. They used to be two descriptions above two buttons, which left you to work out which went with which.

## 1.2

### New: the Holidays pack

Thirty grooves for the days a drummer actually gets called for — Christmas, Halloween, the Fourth of July, New Year, St Patrick's Day and Valentine's Day — filed by the occasion rather than by the feel, so one December gig is in one place. Sleigh-bell shuffles, a toy-drum march and a carol waltz; tom-driven horror and a 7/8 crawl; street beats, drumline cadences and a fife-and-drum with the flams written in. Three seasonal themes come with it: Holly, Pumpkin and Firework. Available as its own purchase.

### PocketCheck hears a kick drum properly

One kick could be counted as two or three hits, and it got worse the more of them you played. It counts once now. Fixed against real recordings of a real kit rather than by guesswork.

### Keep a take's recording

A new switch saves the audio of a PocketCheck take so you can share it. If the app ever mishears your kit, that recording is what makes it fixable.

### Trying colors on

The theme picker stays open when you choose a color, so you can click through them and see each one instead of going back through the menu every time.

## 1.1

The first public release. What it added over the test builds:

**Practice tools**

- Displacement practice: permute or shift the groove every 2, 4, or 8 bars while the notation stays put.
- Sticking fills: tap the R/L label to fill the row with all right, all left, alternating from either hand, or clear.
- A practice clock and bar counter sit in the transport, so you know where you are and how long you've been at it.

**Notation**

- Triplet marks draw on every triplet grid, centred over the beat.
- 5/8 and 7/8 beam in threes and twos.

**Sharing**

- Share links are tappable in chat apps. They open the groove straight into Tating, or take a friend to the right store for their device.

**Fixed**

- Exporting on iPad no longer freezes the app.
- PocketCheck judges a take more fairly, especially quiet endings.
- Purchases behave better offline, and refunds are recognized.
