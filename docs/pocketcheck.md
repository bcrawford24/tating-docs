# PocketCheck

Play the groove on a real kit and see where every hit actually landed against the beat.

## How it reads you

1. **It measures the round trip first.** Before a take, PocketCheck measures the time from the phone's speaker to its microphone, so the reading is honest.
2. **You play.** Every hit is placed against the beat.
3. **It reports placement, not a mark.** Sitting behind the beat is a feel. What matters is how tight the cloud of hits is.

## The microphone

The microphone is used only while the PocketCheck screen is open. The sound is analyzed completely on the phone.

There's one exception you choose: a switch can **keep a take's recording** so you can share it. If the app ever mishears your kit, that recording is what makes it fixable. What a take keeps, and how long, is set out in the [privacy policy](./privacy).
