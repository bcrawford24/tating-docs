# Writing grooves

## Built for fingers

- **Tap a cell** to place a note.
- **Press, hold, and drag** to paint a run of them.
- **Flick up from a cell** for that row's second sound: the open hat under the closed one, the ghost note under the backbeat.
- **Press and hold a note** for that drum's other sounds, with the full list one tap further.

## A drummer's vocabulary

Ghost notes, accents, flams, drags, buzz strokes, cross stick, open, closed and tight hi-hats, hi-hat with the foot, ride and ride bell. Stickings are written under the staff and can be tapped in on a row of their own.

Hand percussion too, from the tom rows: congas, bongos, djembe, clave, woodblocks, shaker, tambourine.

### Stickings in one press

Tap the **R/L** label to fill a sticking row in one press: all right, all left, alternating from either hand, doubles, or the paradiddle.

### The skeleton of a beat

Every row's sheet fills **On**, **Down**, **Up**, **1/3**, **2/4**, **Double**, or **Shuffle**, and each chip presses off again. Snare on 2 and 4 and kick on 1 and 3 is two presses, and a wrong guess is one more.

## Every meter, properly

Time signatures from 4/4 to 7/8, 9/16 and 13/16, on straight or triplet grids, quarter notes down to 32nds. Odd meters are beamed and counted the way drummers group them: 5/8 and 7/8 beam in threes and twos, and triplet marks draw centred over the beat.
