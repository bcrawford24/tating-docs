# Free and paid

## The free version

The free version is a complete tool. Every meter, every grid, playback, the metronome, the count-in, the play-along mutes and the notation view all work without paying, and so do all forty standard rudiments.

What the free version limits is how much you can keep (ten saved grooves) and PocketCheck, which is free for five takes so you can hear it work before deciding.

## The one purchase

One purchase unlocks everything, once, with no subscription:

- An unlimited library, with folders
- The full starter library: grooves, fills, exercises, and the hybrid rudiments
- PocketCheck with no take limit
- Export sheet music as a PDF, a picture for a chat, or a MIDI file for a DAW
- Every color theme

## The Holidays pack

Thirty grooves for the days a drummer actually gets called for (Christmas, Halloween, the Fourth of July, New Year, St Patrick's Day, and Valentine's Day), filed by the occasion rather than by the feel. Three seasonal themes come with it: Holly, Pumpkin, and Firework. It's available as its own purchase.

On the purchase card, the unlock and the Holidays pack each sit above their own button.
