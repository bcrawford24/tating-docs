# Privacy policy

:::note
This is the same text as the policy published at [bcrawford24.github.io/tating-privacy](https://bcrawford24.github.io/tating-privacy/), which the app stores link to.
:::

**Last updated: 7 September 2026**

This policy explains what Tating does with your information. It is written to be
read, not to be survived. It applies the same way whether you installed Tating
from the App Store or from Google Play.

The short version: Tating keeps your grooves on your phone. It has no accounts,
no sign-in, no adverts and no analytics. It does not send your work anywhere.
The one exception is buying things, which goes through the store you installed
Tating from and a billing service called RevenueCat, and that is described in
full below.

## Who is responsible

Tating is published by Robert Benjamin Crawford.

If you want to ask anything about this policy, or about the information Tating
holds, write to **tatingapp@gmail.com**.

## What stays on your phone

All of this is stored on your device and never leaves it unless you send it
somewhere yourself:

- **Your grooves.** Saved grooves, their titles, tags, folders and favourites
  live in a database file inside the app's own storage.
- **Your settings.** Theme, accent colour, and which drum sounds you have
  replaced with your own.
- **Crash reports.** If the app fails, it writes what went wrong — the time, the
  error message and the technical trace — into that same local storage, and
  keeps the last twenty. This is so you can read them and tell us what happened.
  **They are never sent automatically.** Nothing in the app uploads them.

Deleting the app removes all of it from the phone.

One thing to know, because it is your phone's doing rather than Tating's: if
you have your phone's own backup switched on, it may copy Tating's data —
grooves, settings, practice history — into your account's backup along with
every other app's: Google Drive on Android, or iCloud on an iPhone or iPad.
Tating does not put it there and cannot read it; it is your phone maker's
backup of your device, covered by their own terms, and you can turn it off in
your phone's settings. Nothing is sent to us.

## The microphone

Tating's PocketCheck feature listens while you play on a real kit, so it can show
you where your hits landed against the beat. Your phone asks for microphone
permission the first time you use it.

While it is listening, the sound is analysed on your phone, moment by moment, to
work out when a hit happened. **No recording is made.** The audio is not saved to
a file, not kept after the screen is closed, and not sent anywhere.

What is kept is the *result* — for each take, the tempo, how many hits landed and
how many were missed, and how early or late they were on average. No sound, and
nothing about the room. That is stored on your phone so the screen can show you
whether you are getting better, and it stays until you delete the app.

If you never open PocketCheck, the microphone is never used.

## Your own drum sounds

You can point any drum at an audio file of your own. Tating remembers where that
file is on your phone so it can play it. It does not copy the file, upload it, or
look at anything else on your device.

## Sharing and exporting

Everything that leaves Tating leaves because you chose to send it:

- **Sharing a groove** opens your phone's normal share sheet with a link in it.
  You pick who gets it and which app sends it. Tating does not send it.
- **Exporting sheet music (PDF) or a MIDI file** writes the file into the app's
  temporary storage and hands it to the share sheet, so you can save it or send
  it on. Again, you choose the destination.
- **Sending feedback** opens your own email app with a message already addressed
  and with the app version and your phone's operating system version filled in,
  because those are the two things we always have to ask. Nothing is sent until
  you press send in your email app, and you can edit or delete anything in the
  message first. If you send it, we receive your email address, because that is
  how email works.

## Buying things

Tating offers a one-time in-app purchase and a separate one-time content pack.
This is the only part of the app that communicates with anyone.

Purchases are handled by **whichever store you installed Tating from** — the
App Store on an iPhone or iPad, Google Play on Android — and by **RevenueCat**,
a service that keeps track of what has been bought so your purchase still
works when you change phone or reinstall, on either platform.

- **The App Store or Google Play** processes the payment, depending on where
  you got the app. Tating never sees your card details, your name or your
  address. That processing is covered by Apple's or Google's own privacy
  policy, whichever store you used.
- **RevenueCat** receives the purchase and receipt information for the
  transaction, together with technical information about the device — including
  the app version, the operating system version, device model details and the IP
  address the request came from. It creates an **anonymous app user ID** for the
  installation so it can recognise the same purchase later. That ID is generated
  by the service and is not linked to your name, your email address, or your
  Apple or Google account, by Tating.

Tating does not send RevenueCat your grooves, your settings, your crash reports
or anything from the microphone.

Their policies:

- RevenueCat — https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/
- Apple — https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google — https://policies.google.com/privacy

## What Tating does not do

- No user accounts and no sign-in.
- No adverts, and no advertising or tracking software of any kind.
- No analytics: nothing counts your taps, your sessions or your screens.
- No location, contacts, photos, calendar, call or message data.
- Your grooves are never uploaded, backed up to us, or seen by anyone else.
- Nothing is sold or rented to anybody.

## Children

Tating is a practice tool for drummers and is not directed at children under 13.
It does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your rights over your information

Almost everything Tating holds is on your own phone, and you control it directly:
delete a groove, clear the crash log from inside the app, or uninstall the app to
remove all of it.

Practice history is the one thing with no button of its own yet. A take's result
outlives the groove it was played against — deliberately, so a groove you tidy
away does not take your progress with it — and today the way to clear it is to
uninstall. If you would rather it went sooner than that, write to the address
below and say so.

The purchase records held by RevenueCat and by Apple or Google are the
exception. If you want a copy of the purchase records connected to your
installation, or want them deleted, write to **tatingapp@gmail.com** and it
will be dealt with. Deleting purchase records may mean a past purchase can no
longer be restored.

Wherever you live, you can ask what is held about you, ask for it to be
corrected, and ask for it to be deleted. Since Tating keeps nothing on a server
of its own, in practice that means the purchase records above, and the address
to write to is the one above. If you are in the UK or the EU you also have the
right to complain to your national data protection regulator.
