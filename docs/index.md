# What Tating is

Tating is a drum notation editor built for a touchscreen and for drummers.

Tap out a groove on the grid, hear it played back with real sounds, and read it as proper notation. No account, no sign-in, and no signal needed.

**Get it:** [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6802107801) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tating.app)

## Your grooves stay on your phone

Nothing is uploaded. Tating has no accounts, no adverts, and no analytics. The [privacy policy](./privacy) says exactly what it does keep, and where.
