# Reading, saving, and sharing

## Read it

Real notation: noteheads, stems, beams, cross noteheads for cymbals, stems up for hands and down for feet. The grid is for writing; the staff is for reading while you play.

## Keep it

- **Library:** save grooves to a library on the phone, with search, tags, and favorites. The library's filter row includes a **Holidays** chip for the seasonal grooves.
- **Share:** send a groove to another drummer as a link that opens straight into Tating, or takes them to the right store for their device.
- **MIDI:** import MIDI on the standard drum map, and export it with the unlock so grooves move out to a DAW.

## Make it yours

- Point any drum at a recording of your own kit.
- Light or dark, and a choice of accent colors. The theme picker stays open while you try colors on.
